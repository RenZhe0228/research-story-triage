# Universal Research Rubric

Score each candidate direction from 1 to 5 on every dimension. Use the score as a judgment aid, not a substitute for claim-boundary reasoning. Apply hard veto rules after scoring.

## Score Meaning

- 1: Fatal or nearly fatal weakness; cannot support the proposed story.
- 2: Major weakness; usable only as exploratory or heavily downgraded.
- 3: Plausible but incomplete; needs targeted validation or reframing.
- 4: Strong enough for a defensible main line with bounded claims.
- 5: Very strong; clear question, direct evidence, appropriate methods, and coherent publication logic.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question clarity | Lists analyses without a testable question. | Implied question but still broad or mixed. | Clear scientific question with a defined contrast, mechanism, or prediction. |
| Novelty boundary | Repeats known work or prior dataset claims. | Incremental contribution but usable with precise framing. | Distinct contribution with clear boundary from prior work. |
| Evidence closure | Central claim is not directly tested. | Evidence supports part of the claim but leaves important gaps. | Available data directly support the central claim. |
| Method fit | Methods do not answer the question. | Methods answer a simplified version of the question. | Methods match the question and claim strength. |
| Statistical defensibility | n, replication, model design, or validation is inadequate. | Some limits, but a cautious analysis is defensible. | Sample structure, modeling, validation, and uncertainty are appropriate. |
| Causality risk | Correlation is framed as cause. | Causal wording can be removed or bounded. | Design supports causal interpretation, or claims are non-causal. |
| Mechanistic depth | Mechanism is speculative. | Mechanistic interpretation has indirect support. | Mechanism is tested with appropriate data or strong convergent evidence. |
| Figure continuity | Results cannot form a coherent figure sequence. | A story is possible but needs pruning. | Results naturally form a 4-7 figure manuscript. |
| Publication fit | Too weak or mismatched for target output. | Fit is plausible after reframing. | Scope, novelty, evidence, and risk match the target venue or output. |
| Execution feasibility | Required analyses are unavailable or unrealistic. | Finishable with focused extra work. | Finishable with current data, resources, and timeline. |

## Recommended Interpretation

- Weighted average >= 4.0: main-line candidate, unless vetoed.
- Weighted average >= 3.2: secondary/supporting line.
- Weighted average >= 2.4: exploratory only.
- Weighted average < 2.4: unsuitable / kill.

## Hard Vetoes

- Evidence closure <= 2: downgrade at least one class.
- Statistical defensibility <= 2: downgrade at least one class.
- Causal central claim with observational-only data: cannot be main-line.
- Genomic potential used to claim activity, flux, or ecosystem process: cannot be main-line and must be bounded.
- Carbon sink claim without SOC stock, depth, bulk density, baseline, and uncertainty: cannot be main-line.
