# Domain Profiles

Use these profiles to adapt evidence standards across fields. Load the specific profile needed; do not force soil carbon rules onto unrelated disciplines.

## 1. Life Sciences And Ecology

- Typical data: experiments, surveys, traits, abundance, physiology, environmental gradients.
- Evidence standards: replication, controls, uncertainty, mechanism or convergent support.
- Common overclaims: causality from correlation, ecosystem process claims from proxies.
- Validation requirements: independent sites, perturbation, time series, process measurements, or sensitivity checks.
- Figure logic: system context, pattern, mechanism, validation, implication.
- Suitable language: "associated with", "consistent with", "supports the hypothesis".

## 2. Microbiome And Metagenomics

- Typical data: amplicons, metagenomes, MAGs, functional annotation, qPCR, metatranscriptomics.
- Evidence standards: compositional awareness, abundance weighting, contamination checks, functional validation when claiming activity.
- Common overclaims: taxonomy as function, genes as activity, MAG potential as ecosystem process.
- Validation requirements: expression, enzymes, isotope tracing, process rates, independent cohort, or experimental perturbation.
- Figure logic: community structure, function, linkage to host/environment/process, validation.
- Suitable language: "encodes potential", "is enriched", "is associated with".

## 3. Environmental Science

- Typical data: field measurements, gradients, remote sensing, chemistry, hydrology, process rates.
- Evidence standards: sampling design, spatial/temporal representativeness, uncertainty propagation.
- Common overclaims: site-level associations as regional mechanisms.
- Validation requirements: independent sites, temporal baselines, uncertainty, sensitivity analysis.
- Figure logic: study system, gradient, measured response, model or mechanism, uncertainty.
- Suitable language: "varied with", "predicted under", "was estimated with uncertainty".

## 4. Soil Science

- Typical data: SOC, pH, nutrients, texture, bulk density, depth profiles, fractions, enzymes.
- Evidence standards: depth and bulk density for stocks, fraction-specific interpretation, soil covariates.
- Common overclaims: concentration as stock, stability metric as persistence, labile/recalcitrant oversimplification.
- Validation requirements: depth-resolved measurements, density corrections, temporal baseline, fraction or mineral data.
- Figure logic: soil context, stock/fraction response, drivers, uncertainty, implication.
- Suitable language: "SOC concentration", "SOC stock in the measured layer", "stability proxy".

## 5. Climate And Carbon-Cycle Modeling

- Typical data: models, remote sensing, fluxes, inventories, climate and land-use covariates.
- Evidence standards: calibration, validation, uncertainty propagation, scenario boundaries.
- Common overclaims: prediction as mechanism, model fit as truth, regional extrapolation without uncertainty.
- Validation requirements: independent validation, sensitivity analysis, out-of-domain checks.
- Figure logic: data/model setup, calibration, validation, spatial estimate, uncertainty.
- Suitable language: "estimated", "projected", "under the modeled assumptions".

## 6. Agronomy And Land Management

- Typical data: field trials, management history, yield, SOC, nutrients, amendments, tillage.
- Evidence standards: treatment replication, baseline, duration, management comparability.
- Common overclaims: short-term treatment response as permanent sequestration.
- Validation requirements: multi-year data, baseline, depth, bulk density, leakage/additionality for carbon claims.
- Figure logic: practice, measured agronomic/soil response, tradeoffs, feasibility.
- Suitable language: "increased under these trial conditions", "potential co-benefit".

## 7. Medicine And Public Health

- Typical data: cohorts, trials, registries, omics, imaging, surveys.
- Evidence standards: confounding control, clinical relevance, preregistration or validation when applicable.
- Common overclaims: association as efficacy, biomarker as causal driver.
- Validation requirements: external cohort, randomized design, sensitivity analysis, calibration.
- Figure logic: cohort, primary outcome, adjusted analysis, validation, clinical relevance.
- Suitable language: "associated with risk", "predicts in validation data", "requires prospective testing".

## 8. Materials And Chemistry

- Typical data: synthesis, characterization, spectroscopy, microscopy, performance tests.
- Evidence standards: controls, reproducibility, mechanistic characterization, benchmark comparison.
- Common overclaims: performance without durability or mechanism.
- Validation requirements: repeat batches, stability, controls, real-use conditions.
- Figure logic: design, structure, property, mechanism, benchmark, durability.
- Suitable language: "shows enhanced performance under", "consistent with the proposed mechanism".

## 9. Engineering And Technology

- Typical data: prototypes, benchmarks, simulations, system tests, ablations.
- Evidence standards: baselines, reproducibility, constraints, failure modes.
- Common overclaims: narrow benchmark as general solution.
- Validation requirements: independent test cases, ablations, stress tests, deployment constraints.
- Figure logic: architecture, method, benchmark, ablation, limitation.
- Suitable language: "improves on tested benchmarks", "under the evaluated constraints".

## 10. Social Sciences

- Typical data: surveys, interviews, experiments, administrative records, behavioral traces.
- Evidence standards: construct validity, sampling, identification strategy, confounding control.
- Common overclaims: descriptive pattern as causal explanation.
- Validation requirements: identification design, robustness checks, triangulation, external validity.
- Figure logic: theory, data, identification, result, robustness, implication.
- Suitable language: "is associated with", "suggests", "under this identification strategy".

## 11. Computational And AI Research

- Typical data: models, algorithms, datasets, benchmarks, user studies, ablations.
- Evidence standards: strong baselines, dataset leakage checks, ablation, reproducibility.
- Common overclaims: benchmark gains as general intelligence or broad real-world utility.
- Validation requirements: held-out datasets, leakage audit, ablation, error analysis, robustness.
- Figure logic: task, method, benchmark, ablation, qualitative/quantitative error, efficiency.
- Suitable language: "improves on", "generalizes to the tested held-out setting".
